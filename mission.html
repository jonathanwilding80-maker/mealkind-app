Our mission

Nobody should heal hungry.

Good food is part of recovery. MealKind exists to bring food security, nourishing meals and simple nutrition know-how to people going through treatment and recovery, whether or not they have family and friends around them.

Why it matters

23 vs 15hospital readmissions within 30 days, per 100 stays, for patients with and without malnutrition
50%+higher readmission rate when a patient is malnourished

Source: Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality, HCUP Statistical Brief #218 (US hospital data).

Treatment can take away appetite, energy and the ability to shop and cook. The people around a patient want to help but often don't know what's safe to bring. MealKind's Care Circles solve that for families who have support. MealKind Social is how we reach those who don't.
