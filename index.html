Two ways to use MealKind

Less guessing. Better support.

Patient experience

For patients & families

  • Menus and recipes written for the condition and screened for allergies and foods to avoid.
  • A weekly meal plan with a shopping list that skips what's already in the fridge.
  • One page that shows who's bringing dinner, driving to appointments or helping at home.
  • Messages and photos from the people who care, with private notes for the family.
Support experience

For supporters

  • See exactly what's needed and pick a date: a meal, a ride, a school pickup, the lawn or the dogs.
  • Know what to cook: allergies, foods to avoid and what's already planned are right there.
  • Get a text reminder before your turn, with drop-off details.
  • Send a message, or help from afar with groceries or a cleaning fund.
Every stage of care

Here for the whole journey.

Before

Plan ahead for a surgery, a new diagnosis or a new baby. Stock the freezer and line up help before you need it.

During

Menus that match how they're feeling that week, and a Care Circle that covers meals, rides and the everyday jobs.

After

Meals that support recovery and the return to everyday routines, for as long as it takes.

Who we are

About MealKind

MealKind was born from personal experience of how hard it is to care for someone you love, and how much the right help at the right time can mean. We built it to pass that support forward to our community. Based in San Antonio, Texas, MealKind is a patient navigation platform for food and support. We combine condition-specific menus with practical care coordination, so patients eat well before, during and after care, and the families, friends and communities around them can offer support that is organized, consistent and useful. MealKind is available in English and Spanish.

Before you start

Your privacy, in plain words.

MealKind handles personal moments. Here's exactly what we keep and who can see it.

What we keep

Names you enter, the circle's food notes (allergies, likes, drop-off details), the dates people sign up for, and messages or photos you post. If you ask for reminders, your mobile number or email.

Who sees it

Anyone with the circle's link sees names, dates and what's being brought. Phone numbers and emails are never shown to other members. The drop-off address only appears after someone signs up. Private notes go only to the family.

What we never do

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. We only use it to run your Care Circle and send the reminders you asked for.

Health and food

MealKind isn't a medical service. Menus and recipes are general guidance; follow your care team's advice. Only share what you're comfortable sharing.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to stop reminders. Email hello@mealkind.com any time to have a circle or your details deleted.
