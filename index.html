Patient navigation for food and recovery

Food is how we show up.

When someone we love is unwell, we all want to help, but it's hard to know what they need, and hard for them to ask. MealKind takes the guesswork out of what to cook during illness and recovery. Every meal is matched to their specific illness and dietary needs, from dialysis to chemotherapy to heart health, and built on published dietitian guidelines, so the people who care can help with food that truly helps. Choose the recipes, share one link, and let family and friends sign up to cook.

Our mission

Nobody should heal hungry.

Hospital patients with malnutrition are readmitted more than 50% more often. Through MealKind Social we're building a way to bring nourishing meals to people going through treatment who don't have support.

About MealKind

MealKind was born from personal experience of how hard it is to care for someone you love, and how much the right help at the right time can mean. We built it to pass that support forward to our community.

Based in San Antonio, Texas, MealKind is a patient navigation platform for food and support. We combine condition-specific recipes with simple meal coordination, so patients eat well before, during and after care, and the people around them can help in a way that is organized and useful.
